Our approach

Designing spatial botanical systems

Botanical Dubai is a Dubai-based botanical and spatial design studio. We work with living plants as a material (grown, not manufactured), shaping spaces for retail, F&B, hospitality, brands and private clients.

Our work spans living installations, event activations and workshops, through to table-scapes and botanical gifting: from a permanent architectural centrepiece to a hands-on session or a single stem.

Each installation is built as a spatial composition, not a product.
