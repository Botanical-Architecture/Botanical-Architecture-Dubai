Services

Pop-ups

Workshops

The Botanical Bar

Our current spatial activation is Midsummer Florals, an interactive design pop-up hosted in the minimalist setting of Vera Beauty and Wellness in Jumeirah.

Guests are invited to step up to the bar, select curated raw stems, and construct their own custom bouquet. The experience is accompanied by specialty matcha pairings.
